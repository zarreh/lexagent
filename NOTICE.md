# NOTICE

This is an independent, clean-room implementation.

The **problem framing** — legal-reasoning assistance over landlord-tenant
statutes and case law — was inspired by problems studied in graduate
agentic-AI coursework. No course code, notebooks, datasets, databases, slides
or other course materials are reproduced or redistributed here.

- All source code is written from scratch against the published behaviour of the
  problem, not against any course implementation.
- Course material used only as a local reference lives under `reference/`, which
  is excluded from version control and must never be committed or published.
- The legal citations are drawn from **public-domain statute text**: the Texas
  Property Code Chapter 92 and the California Civil Code §§1940–1954, available
  from the Texas Legislature and the California Legislative Information websites.
- Case summaries and outcomes are **synthetic precedent** produced by a seeded
  generator (`data/seed_precedents.py`). They are not real court opinions and do
  not represent any real case, court, or party.
- Labelled legal scenarios are synthetic and generated by `data/scenarios.py`.

No course provider endorses this work.
